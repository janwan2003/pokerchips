About EasyPoker

Last updated 2026-11-12

EasyPoker (easypoker.cc) is a free, browser-based poker chip tracker for live home games. The host creates a table, players join from their own phones with a link or QR code, and every bet, pot and payout updates on every phone at once. Players still deal a real deck of cards.

What it does

  • Keeps each player's chip stack on their own phone, with a live shared pot.
  • Lets the host set the starting stack and the blinds (presets from 1/2 to 1,000/2,000) and optionally allow buy-ins of a fixed amount.
  • Seats up to 30 players at one table.
  • Saves every action on the server as it happens, so a closed tab or dead battery loses nothing.
  • Lets the host correct any stack or the pot, change blinds, remove a player, and restore the table to any earlier moment.

What it does not do

  • It does not deal cards or decide who wins a hand.
  • It does not work out side pots automatically.
  • It does not handle money, sell chips, or offer real-money gambling.

Cost and accounts

Free. There are no accounts, no app download and no ads. See the privacy page for exactly what is stored.

Not the same as

EasyPoker at easypoker.cc is an independent project. It is not affiliated with the "Poker with Friends – EasyPoker" mobile app or the easy.poker website, which is a separate product that deals virtual cards.
