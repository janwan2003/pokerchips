Betting and the pot

All-in
Betting every chip you have left.
Ante
A small forced bet every player puts in before a hand, used in some games and late tournament levels.
Bet
The first chips put in during a betting round.
Big blind
The larger of the two forced bets, posted by the player two seats left of the button.
Buy-in
The amount you pay or receive to start playing, or to start again after losing your chips.
Call
Matching the current bet.
Check
Passing without betting when nobody has bet yet in the round.
Fold
Giving up your hand and any chips you have already put in.
Main pot
The pot every player in the hand can win, including a player who is all-in.
Pot
All the chips bet in the current hand, won by the best hand or the last player left.
Raise
Matching the current bet and adding more.
Re-buy
Buying more chips after running out, if the game allows it.
Side pot
A separate pot for chips an all-in player could not match; only players who contributed to it can win it.
Small blind
The smaller forced bet, posted by the player directly left of the button.
Stack
The chips a player has in front of them.
Straddle
An optional, voluntary blind of twice the big blind, posted before the cards are dealt.

Cards and the deal

Board
The community cards face up in the middle of the table.
Burn card
The card discarded face down before the flop, turn and river are dealt.
Button
The marker showing who is the dealer for the hand; it moves one seat left every hand.
Community cards
Cards dealt face up that every player can use.
Flop
The first three community cards, dealt together.
Hole cards
Your private cards, dealt face down.
Kicker
A card that is not part of your made hand but breaks ties.
Muck
The pile of folded and discarded cards; also, to throw your hand away.
River
The fifth and last community card.
Showdown
When the remaining players reveal their hands to decide who wins.
Turn
The fourth community card.

Hands and strategy

Bluff
Betting with a weak hand to make better hands fold.
Draw
A hand that needs more cards to become strong, such as four cards to a flush.
Nuts
The best possible hand given the board.
Outs
The cards left in the deck that would improve your hand to a likely winner.
Pocket pair
Two hole cards of the same rank.
Set
Three of a kind made with a pocket pair and one matching board card.
Tilt
Playing badly because you are frustrated or angry.
Wheel
The lowest straight: 5-4-3-2-A.

Positions at the table

Cutoff
The seat directly right of the button.
Early position
The first seats to act before the flop, which act with the least information.
In position
Acting after your opponent in a betting round, which is an advantage.
Under the gun
The player directly left of the big blind, who acts first before the flop.

Formats

Cash game
A game where chips stand for a fixed amount and players can join, re-buy or leave at any time.
Chop
Splitting a pot or a tournament prize pool by agreement.
No-limit
Betting rules where you may bet any amount up to your whole stack.
Tournament
A game with rising blinds where players are eliminated until one has every chip.

Frequently asked questions

What does "the nuts" mean in poker?

The best possible hand given the community cards on the board.

What is the difference between the flop, turn and river?

The flop is the first three community cards, the turn is the fourth and the river is the fifth and last.

What does "under the gun" mean?

The player directly to the left of the big blind, who acts first before the flop.

What is a kicker?

A card that is not part of your pair or other made hand, used to break ties between equal hands.
