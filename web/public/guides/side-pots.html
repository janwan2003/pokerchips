How side pots work in poker

A side pot is created when a player goes all-in for less than the others are betting. The all-in player can only win the main pot, which holds what they could match from each player; everything above that goes into a side pot that only the other players can win.

Last updated 2026-11-12

The rule in one sentence

A player can only win from each opponent as much as they themselves put in. Side pots are simply the bookkeeping for that rule.

Worked example: three players, one short stack

Ana has 100 chips, Ben has 300, Cleo has 500. Ana goes all-in for 100, Ben calls and raises to 300 (all-in), Cleo calls 300.

PotHow it is builtSizeWho can win it
Main pot100 from each of Ana, Ben, Cleo300Ana, Ben or Cleo
Side potThe next 200 from each of Ben and Cleo400Ben or Cleo only

Cleo bet 300 and keeps her other 200; nothing above 300 goes in because nobody can match it.

At showdown, compare hands for each pot separately, starting with the side pot:

  • If Ana has the best hand overall, she wins the main pot (300). The better of Ben and Cleo wins the side pot (400).
  • If Ben has the best hand, he wins both pots (700).
  • If Cleo beats Ben but loses to Ana: Ana takes 300, Cleo takes 400.

How to build side pots step by step

  1. Find the smallest all-in amount. Take that much from every player in the hand: that is the main pot.
  2. From what is left, find the next-smallest all-in amount and take that much from every player who still has chips in: that is side pot 1.
  3. Repeat until every chip is in a pot. Any amount a single player put in that nobody could match goes back to that player.
  4. Each pot is won by the best hand among the players who contributed to it.

Two all-ins: a second example

Four players. Dev is all-in for 50, Eli is all-in for 150, Fay and Gus both bet 400.

PotCalculationSizeEligible
Main pot50 × 4200Dev, Eli, Fay, Gus
Side pot 1(150 − 50) × 3300Eli, Fay, Gus
Side pot 2(400 − 150) × 2500Fay, Gus

Total 1,000, which matches 50 + 150 + 400 + 400. Always check the pots add up to everything that was bet.

Paying side pots out with EasyPoker

EasyPoker keeps one pot on the table and does not split it into side pots automatically. Work out the pots as above, then pay them out in order: the winner of each smaller pot taps Take and enters that pot's size, and the last winner taps Take whole pot for whatever is left. If anyone takes the wrong amount, the host can set the pot and the stacks directly, or restore the table to the moment before.

Frequently asked questions

What is a side pot in poker?

A separate pot for chips that an all-in player could not match. Only the players who put chips into a side pot can win it.

Can an all-in player win more than they bet?

They can win the main pot, which holds their own all-in plus the same amount from every caller, but nothing from the side pots.

Who wins the side pot if the all-in player has the best hand?

The all-in player takes the main pot; the side pot goes to the best hand among the other players who contributed to it.

How do you calculate a side pot?

Take the smallest all-in amount from every player for the main pot, then repeat with the next all-in amount for each side pot, until all chips are assigned.
