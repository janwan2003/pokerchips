What are virtual poker chips?

Virtual poker chips (also called digital poker chips) replace the physical chip set in a live poker game. You still deal real cards and play at a real table. The only thing that moves to the phones is the money: each player sees their own stack, taps to bet, and the pot updates on every phone at the same moment.

They are not play money for an online poker site, and nothing is bought or sold. They are a scoreboard for the chips you would otherwise push across the felt.

How virtual chips work at EasyPoker

  1. The host creates a table at EasyPoker and picks the starting stack and the blinds (for example 1,000 chips with 5/10 blinds).
  2. Everyone joins by scanning the QR code on the host's screen or opening the shared link, then types a name. No app to install, no account.
  3. Players bet from their own phone. Pick an amount, tap Bet, and the chips leave your stack and land in the pot.
  4. The winner taps Take whole pot (or takes part of it for a split pot). Every phone shows the new stacks instantly.

Every action is saved the moment it happens. If a phone dies or someone closes the tab, they open the link again and their stack is exactly where they left it.

Virtual chips vs. a physical chip set

Physical chipsVirtual chips
CostA decent 300 to 500 chip set costs real moneyFree
SetupCount and hand out stacks by colourUnder a minute: share one link
Making changeConstant, and slows every handNever: bet any exact amount
Counting the potBy eye, with mistakesAlways exact, on every phone
MistakesHard to prove, hard to undoHost can fix any stack or rewind
TravelHeavy case, chips go missingNothing to carry
FeelThe clack of chips is greatYou lose the shuffle and the stacking

Physical chips still win on feel. Virtual chips win on everything that slows a home game down.

When digital chips make the most sense

  • You do not own a chip set, or it is at someone else's house.
  • You are travelling: holidays, cabins, camping, a hotel room. A deck of cards fits in a pocket; a chip case does not.
  • The group is big. Eight or nine players drain a 300-chip set fast, and colours run out.
  • You keep losing track of rebuys, side bets or who put what in the pot.
  • New players are at the table. Nobody needs to learn what a green chip is worth.

What virtual chips do not do

EasyPoker does not deal cards and does not handle real money. Deal a normal deck at the table, and settle any real stakes between yourselves at the end of the night if your group plays for them. The app only tracks the chips.

Frequently asked questions

Are virtual poker chips free?

Yes. Creating a table and joining one is free, with no account and no app download.

Do all players need the app?

No. Everyone plays in their phone browser by opening the table link or scanning the QR code.

What happens if my phone battery dies?

Every action is saved on the server as it happens. Open the link again on any device, enter the same name, and your stack is still there.

Can the host correct a mistake?

Yes. The host can set any player's stack, set the pot, change the blinds, remove a player, or restore the table to any earlier moment.

How many players can join?

A normal home game of two to ten players works well. Each player only needs a phone with a browser.
