Two very different things called "online poker chips"

When people search for online poker chips they usually mean one of two things:

  • Play-money chips on poker sites and social casino games. These are a site's own currency for its online card tables. EasyPoker has nothing to do with those, and does not sell chips of any kind.
  • Chips for a real game with friends, kept online. You play with a physical deck, and an online table keeps everyone's stack in sync on their phones. This page is about that.

Why keep your chips online?

  • Every phone shows the same numbers. A bet on one phone shows up on all the others at once.
  • Nothing is lost. Each action is saved on the server as it happens. A phone can die, a tab can close, Wi-Fi can drop: reopen the link and the stack is still there.
  • The whole night is on record. The host can see every move and restore the table to an earlier point if something went wrong.
  • No equipment. A deck of cards and the phones you already carry are enough.

How to start an online chip table

  1. Open EasyPoker and choose a starting stack and blinds.
  2. Share the table link in your group chat, or show the QR code.
  3. Each friend opens it, types a name and gets their chips.
  4. Deal a real deck. Bet, call and raise from your phones.

Playing poker with friends who are not in the room

Because the chips are online, the table link works from anywhere. The tricky part of a remote game is the cards, not the chips: everyone needs private hole cards. EasyPoker only tracks chips and does not deal cards, so for a remote game you need your own way of dealing, and EasyPoker keeps the money side honest. For games around one table, the setup above is all you need.

What it costs

Nothing. There is no account, no subscription and no in-game purchase. The chips have no cash value: if your group plays for real stakes, settle up between yourselves at the end of the night.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy online poker chips here?

No. EasyPoker does not sell chips. It tracks chips for a game you play with friends using a real deck of cards.

Do online poker chips work on any phone?

Yes. EasyPoker runs in the browser on iPhone, Android and laptops, all at the same table.

Can friends join from different places?

Yes, the table link works from anywhere with an internet connection. EasyPoker does not deal cards, so a remote game needs its own way to deal private hands.

Are the chips saved if we stop and continue later?

Yes. The table and its full history are kept, so you can come back to the same link later.
