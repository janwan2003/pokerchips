Poker chip apps for home games, compared

Several free apps replace a physical chip set while you play with real cards. They differ mainly in whether players must install an app, whether side pots are automatic, and whether the app works out who owes whom at the end.

Which one to pick

  • Mixed iPhone and Android group, nobody wants to install anything: a browser app. EasyPoker joins with a QR code and a name.
  • Lots of all-ins and side pots: an app with automatic side pots saves arithmetic. With EasyPoker you work side pots out by hand (see side pots explained) and each winner takes their share.
  • Cash game where the main job is who owes whom: an app with a settlement feature, such as PartyPot's.
  • The host needs to undo mistakes: EasyPoker keeps every action and lets the host restore the table to any earlier moment.

What all of them have in common

  • You still deal a real deck; none of these replace the cards or the dealer.
  • Each player sees their own stack on their own phone, and the pot updates live.
  • None of them handle real money in a home game. Settle any stakes between yourselves.

What makes a poker chip app good at a real table

  1. Joining in under a minute. If guests have to download and register, the night starts late.
  2. Exact, visible pot. Everyone sees the same number.
  3. Mistakes are fixable without restarting the game.
  4. Nothing is lost when a phone dies or a tab closes.
  5. Works on every phone at the table, iPhone and Android alike.

Not to be confused with

EasyPoker at easypoker.cc is an independent browser chip tracker. It is not the "Poker with Friends – EasyPoker" mobile app, which deals virtual cards and is a separate product from a different developer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free poker chip app?

It depends on the group. For no installs on mixed phones, a browser app like EasyPoker. For automatic side pots or a who-owes-whom settlement, an installed app with that feature.

Is there a poker chip app that works without downloading?

Yes. EasyPoker runs in the phone browser: the host shares a link or QR code and players join with a name.

Do poker chip apps handle side pots?

Some do automatically. EasyPoker does not: you work the side pots out and each winner takes their share of the pot.

Is easypoker.cc the same as the EasyPoker app?

No. easypoker.cc is an independent browser chip tracker for games played with real cards. The "Poker with Friends – EasyPoker" app is a separate product.
