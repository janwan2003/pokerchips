How it works

  1. One person creates a table and shares the link or QR code.
  2. Everyone opens it on their phone and types a name.
  3. Bet, take from the pot, take the whole pot. Every phone updates live.

Every action is saved, so a closed tab or a dead battery loses nothing. The host can correct balances and rewind to any earlier moment.
