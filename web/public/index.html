EasyPoker

Bring the cards.
We bring the chips.

Everyone's stack on their own phone. Host a table in your browser, share the link, deal. No app, no sign-up.

  1. Create a table and show the QR code, or send the link.
  2. Everyone opens it on their phone and types a name.
  3. Bet, take, take the whole pot. Every phone updates at once.

Every action is saved as it happens, so a closed tab or a flat battery loses nothing. The host can fix any balance and rewind to any earlier moment.
