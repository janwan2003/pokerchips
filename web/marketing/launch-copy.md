### Long description

EasyPoker replaces the chip set at a live home poker game. You still deal a real deck;
the chips live on everyone's phone.

The host creates a table in the browser, sets starting chips and blinds, and shows a QR
code or sends a link. Each player opens it on their own phone and types a name: no app,
no account. Bets, the pot and payouts update on every phone at once.

- Up to 30 players per table
- Blind presets from 1/2 to 1,000/2,000, optional fixed buy-ins
- Every action saved on the server: a closed tab or a dead battery loses nothing
- The host can correct any stack or the pot and rewind the table to any earlier moment
- Free, no ads, play chips only (no real money)

It does not deal cards, decide who wins, or split side pots automatically.

## Reddit (r/homegames, or r/poker's weekly thread; check each sub's rules first)

**Title:** I built a free browser chip tracker for home games: everyone's stack on their own phone

**Body:**

We kept losing poker nights to "who owes what" and nobody owning a decent chip set, so I
made a web page that does the chips: the host opens easypoker.cc, sets stacks and blinds,
and everyone joins by QR code on their own phone. Real cards stay on the table.

No app or sign-up, free, no ads, play chips only. Every action is saved, so a dead phone
just reopens the link, and the host can fix a mistake or rewind.

It doesn't deal or work out side pots. I'd love feedback from people who run regular
games: what would make you use it next game night?
